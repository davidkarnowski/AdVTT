AdVTT player demo

Native HTML5 player with native captions from .captions.vtt, ad blocks from .ads.vtt or .advtt.json. The page marks ads and can skip them during playback; it never modifies the media file.

Drop files here: the media file, .captions.vtt (or .captions.srt), and optionally .ads.vtt or .advtt.json. Several files at once are routed by suffix.
